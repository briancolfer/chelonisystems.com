Scute: Work Hours Tracker

Support

Scute tracks your working hours against each contract's weekly cap, and tells you, while you're clocked in, when you'll reach it.

Contact us

The quickest way is Settings → Email Support in Scute. It opens an email to us with your Scute version already in it.

Or write to support@chelonisystems.com. If you can, say which version of Scute you have (it's at the bottom of Scute's Settings) and what you expected to happen.

Getting started

  1. Add your first contract. The first time you open Scute, enter your client's name, say whether it's a 40-hour week (or enter its weekly cap), and add an hourly rate if you want earnings worked out.
  2. Check your regular hours. Scute starts you on weekdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with reminders to clock in and out. Change them in Settings, under your contract.
  3. Set your pay week. If your client's week doesn't start on Monday, change it in Settings, under Pay week.
  4. Clock in. On Log Time, tap Clock In. Scute counts your hours against the cap and shows when to clock out to reach it.

Questions

How do I log time I've already worked?

On Log Time, choose the date, then a start and an end, and tap Log Time. To change an entry later, tap it on the Week tab; to delete it, swipe it to the left.

How do I switch between contracts?

Tap the contract's name at the top of Log Time and choose another. Week and Reports have the same menu at the top. Add more contracts, or projects of your own, in Settings.

What's the difference between a contract and a project?

A contract is client work with a weekly hour cap and an hourly rate. A project is your own work, tracked without a cap or a rate.

My cap or rate changed. How do I update it without changing past weeks?

In Settings, open the contract and change its cap or rate from the pay week it starts in. Weeks before that keep the old cap and rate, so past timesheets and earnings stay right.

What happens to hours over my cap?

They're kept and shown as over the cap. Scute never hides or deletes hours. It warns you as you near the cap (at 80%) and when you reach it.

I don't see the countdown on my Lock Screen.

The countdown shows only while you're clocked in. Check that Lock Screen countdown is on in Scute's Settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for Scute in iOS Settings, under Scute.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Scute in iOS Settings, under Notifications, and that the contract's regular hours and reminders are on in Scute's Settings.

I forgot to clock out.

If a timer runs longer than you'd expect (10 hours unless you change it in Settings), Scute reminds you. When you clock out after that, Scute lets you set the real end time, so the hours you didn't work aren't recorded.

How do I send a timesheet to my client?

On the Week tab, share the week as a spreadsheet (CSV). On the Reports tab, share any period as a PDF or a spreadsheet. To put your name and contact details at the top of PDFs, add them in Settings, under Your details.

Can I use Scute on an iPad or a Mac, or sync between devices?

Not yet. Scute 1.0 is for iPhone, and your hours stay on your iPhone. When you move to a new iPhone, restoring it from a backup brings your Scute data with it.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Only on your iPhone: Scute has no account and sends nothing to us. Deleting the app deletes your data. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Requirements

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
