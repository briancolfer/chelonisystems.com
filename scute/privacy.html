Scute

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Scute collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else unless you choose to send it.

This policy covers Scute: Work Hours Tracker, the iPhone app made by Cheloni Systems ("we").

What Scute keeps on your iPhone

To do its job, Scute stores the following on your iPhone. None of it leaves your iPhone on its own.

  • Your contracts and projects: their names and billing names, weekly hour caps, hourly rates and the weeks they apply from, start and end dates, and your regular work hours and reminder choices.
  • Your hours: when each stretch of work started and ended, and any note you added.
  • Your settings: the day your pay week starts, which contract is selected, when to warn about a timer left running, the report period you last chose, and whether the Lock Screen countdown is on.
  • Your details, if you add them: the name, email address and postal address you choose to print at the top of PDF reports. These are optional.

What Scute doesn't do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It doesn't send your data to us, to advertisers, or to any other company.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

When something leaves Scute: only when you choose

  • Sharing a report or timesheet. When you share a PDF report or a spreadsheet (CSV), iOS lets you choose where it goes. It goes only there, and it includes your details if you added them.
  • Emailing support. Email Support in Settings opens your email app with a message to support@chelonisystems.com. It adds Scute's version and build number so we know which version you have. Nothing is sent until you send it. We use what you send only to answer you, and we don't share it.
  • Your iPhone's backups. Like other apps' data, Scute's is included in the backups your iPhone makes to iCloud or to a computer, if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by us.
  • Crash reports from Apple. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers (in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

What shows on your screen

Reminders, the warnings as you near your weekly cap, and the Lock Screen countdown can show a contract's name and your hours on the Lock Screen, where someone holding your iPhone could see them. You can change how notifications appear in iOS Settings, under Notifications, Scute, and turn off the Lock Screen countdown in Scute's Settings.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your iPhone, deleting Scute deletes it. Backups made before you deleted Scute may still contain it until they're replaced or deleted.

Children

Scute is a work tool and isn't directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If Scute ever starts keeping or sending data somewhere other than your iPhone (for example, syncing between your devices through your own iCloud account), we'll say so here, and in the App Store listing, before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@chelonisystems.com.
